// About Me

Profile Photo

Cybersecurity Professional & SOC Analyst

I'm a passionate cybersecurity professional with 5+ years of experience working in Security Operations Centers, specializing in threat detection, incident response, and digital forensics. My journey in cybersecurity started with a deep curiosity about how systems work — and how they can break.

Currently, I monitor and analyze security events across enterprise environments using industry-leading SIEM platforms like Splunk and IBM QRadar. I've led threat hunting operations, conducted digital forensic investigations, and developed automated detection rules that have identified sophisticated threat actors.

When I'm not in the SOC, you'll find me contributing to open-source security tools, creating CTF challenges, writing technical blog posts, or participating in bug bounty programs. I believe in continuous learning and giving back to the cybersecurity community.
